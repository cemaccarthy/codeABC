Instrument samples for codeABC
Classical (nylon) and steel-string guitar: real recordings from tonejs-instruments (Makefully-Studios/tonejs-instruments,
  MIT licence), trimmed to the attack, faded, normalised and renamed by MIDI note. The steel guitar's notes above D5
  are rendered from GeneralUser GS (program 25) and level-matched.
Harpsichord, electric piano, pizzicato strings, vibraphone, marimba, xylophone:
  rendered note by note from GeneralUser GS v1.471 by S. Christian Collins (http://www.schristiancollins.com),
  licence in GeneralUser-GS-LICENSE.txt. Rendered with spessasynth_core at 44.1 kHz stereo, with the
  soundfont's reverb and chorus off, then encoded as VBR MP3 (about 190 kbps).
  Folders: sounds/<instrument>/v<velocity>/<MIDI note>.mp3, one sample every minor third.
Clavichord: a physical model written for codeABC (two tangent-struck waveguide strings and a soundboard),
  rendered to samples the same way.
Violin, cello and double bass (bowed): solo recordings from tonejs-instruments (MIT), 16-bit WAV originals,
  trimmed to the attack, each given a seamless sustain loop (the steadiest ~1 s of the bow stroke, slow level
  drift evened out, a whole number of pitch periods, 100 ms crossfade), re-encoded as VBR MP3.
Viola (bowed) and violin/cello pizzicato: VSCO-2 Community Edition by Sam Gossner (CC0), via the tiny-orchestra
  package; the viola looped the same way. Viola pizzicato uses the violin pizzicato, and the cello's below G3.
Double bass pizzicato: GeneralUser GS "Acoustic Bass" (a plucked upright bass), rendered with spessasynth.
Flute, clarinet, bassoon: solo recordings from tonejs-instruments (MIT), WAV originals, looped and encoded as
  the bowed strings are. Oboe and the bassoon's lowest notes (B-flat1, C-sharp2, F2): VSCO-2 Community Edition (CC0),
  via tiny-orchestra, looped the same way.
